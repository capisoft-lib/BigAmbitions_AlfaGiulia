[h1]Alfa Romeo Giulia Quadrifoglio (2016) — 1.0.6[/h1]
Drive the Italian sports saloon in Big Ambitions.

[h2]Where to buy[/h2]
Purchase or order at General US Trucks in Industry City or The Hamptons Axis. Price: $79,900. Cargo capacity: 4.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]2.9-litre twin-turbo V6: 375 kW / 510 PS and 600 Nm target torque curve.
[*]Rear-wheel drive, eight-speed automatic gearbox and 307 km/h limiter.
[*]1620 kg, 58 L fuel tank and selectable body colour.
[*]Native steering, suspension, braking, fuel and damage.
[*]Headlights, tail lights, brake lights, reverse lights and left/right indicators using the native blink phase.
[/list]
Performance figures are configuration targets, not measured in-game results. Engine sound comes from the native donor vehicle. Price and cargo capacity are gameplay choices.

[h2]New in version 1.0.3[/h2]
[list]
[*]Compatible with all three private-driver contracts: drop off your owned car at the service garage, then summon your driver for a journey.
[*]The model also appears in ambient traffic with its own body, animated wheels and working lights.
[*]Contract fees and vehicle limits retain the game's normal rules.
[*]The Giulia, Skyline R34 and GT-R R35 can be used together or independently.
[/list]
After updating, restart the game and reload your city. Ambient appearances are random and may take some time.

[h2]Installation[/h2]
Subscribe, let Steam finish downloading and enable the mod in the game. If AlfaGiulia is already installed manually, keep a single installation to avoid duplicates. No additional Workshop item is required; Harmony is bundled.
